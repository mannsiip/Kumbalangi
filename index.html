KUMBALANGI

A village shaped by water, people and tradition.

Kumbalangi is more than a tourist destination. It is a living community where fishing, farming, coir-making, food traditions and backwater life have shaped generations.

Explore Kumbalangi

India’s First Eco-Tourism Village

With a population of over 1 lakh, Kumbalangi is home to farmers, labourers, fishermen, coir spinners and toddy tappers. The main occupation of the villagers is fishing and there are over 100 Chinese fishing nets dotting the backwaters facing the village.
