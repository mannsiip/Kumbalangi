🎬 Kumbalangi in Cinema

Kumbalangi’s landscapes and village setting became more widely known through the 2019 critically acclaimed Malayalam film Kumbalangi Nights.

Kumbalangi Nights

The story is set in the picturesque coastal island village of Kumbalangi near Kochi, Kerala. It follows the turbulent, love-hate dynamics among four estranged brothers—Saji, Boney, Bobby, and Franky—who live together in a run-down house.
