📊 What We Learned

We listened before we acted.

🎣 Fishing is changing — Traditional fishing is becoming less common.
🧺 Coir-making is declining — Younger people are choosing other employment.
🎓 Education has opened new opportunities
🌊 The waterways face concerns — Mud/sedimentation and reduced depth raised by residents.
🌴 Tourism is changing — Opportunities exist, but responsibility is essential.
