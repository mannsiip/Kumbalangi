🎣 Livelihoods of Kumbalangi

The story behind the work that has sustained this village for generations.

Kerala Tourism identifies fishing, farming, coir-making and toddy tapping as traditional livelihoods associated with Kumbalangi. These are not “attractions” — they are the backbone of community life.

Coir Making

Processing coconut husk into fibre, ropes and mats — a skill passed down through generations.

Why it is declining: Community members told us that younger people are choosing other employment. The traditional process is labour-intensive.

What happens when traditional livelihoods disappear? Skills, local identity and economic options shrink.

What happens when traditional livelihoods disappear?

When fishing, coir-making or other ancestral skills fade, the village loses more than income sources. It loses knowledge, intergenerational continuity and a distinctive identity that tourism itself markets. Responsible tourism should support — not replace — these livelihoods.
