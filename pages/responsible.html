♻️ Be a Responsible Visitor

Tourism should reduce ecological and social harm while increasing benefits for local communities.

✅ DO

  • ✅ Carry reusable bottles
  • ✅ Avoid plastic and littering
  • ✅ Ask before photographing residents
  • ✅ Respect fishing activities and private spaces
  • ✅ Don’t disturb mangroves
  • ✅ Support local experiences responsibly
  • ✅ Keep waterways clean
  • ✅ Follow local guidance on night visits for kavaru

❌ DON’T

  • ❌ Throw waste into backwaters
  • ❌ Enter private areas or farms without permission
  • ❌ Disturb fishermen or their nets
  • ❌ Damage mangroves or aquatic habitats
  • ❌ Assume natural phenomena like kavaru will always occur
  • ❌ Force the glow by excessive disturbance of water
  • ❌ Treat the village only as a photo backdrop

Responsible tourism in Kerala explicitly aims to reduce ecological and social harm while increasing benefits for local communities. Your choices as a visitor matter.

Created as part of a college Social Service Responsibility project
